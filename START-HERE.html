2. Preview it first

1Unzip the folder.
2Double-click index.html — it opens in your browser. That is your site.
3The burgundy envelope appears first — click it once to open it and reveal the site.

3. Make it yours

Open index.html in any text editor (Notepad, TextEdit). Every spot you must change is marked with an <!-- EDIT: … --> comment. The main ones:

ANames — search for Vivienne and Alexander (also the V · A monogram in the nav, the envelope, and the footer) and replace with your names & initials.
BDate & venue — replace September 18, 2027 and The Beaumont Manor everywhere.
CCountdown — open script.js, find 2027-09-18T16:00:00 and set your wedding date & time (format: YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS).
DYour story — rewrite the paragraphs in the Our Story and Love Story sections; adjust the Schedule times, Registry links, Dress Code and FAQ answers.
EColors — open styles.css, find the :root block at the top: --ivory, --burgundy, --ink, --gold, --blush. Change the codes to re-theme the whole site at once.

4. The envelope intro

Your guests first see a burgundy envelope sealed with your monogram — one tap opens the flap, the invitation card slides out, and the site is revealed. To change the names or monogram on it, find the <!-- EDIT: envelope intro --> comment at the very top of index.html.

Prefer to skip it? Delete the whole <div id="envelope-intro">…</div> block in index.html and the "ENVELOPE INTRO" section at the top of script.js. The site will load straight into the hero.

6. Get RSVPs in your email ★ important

1In script.js, find const RSVP_EMAIL = "your@email.com" and put your email address between the quotes. Save.
2Open index.html in your browser, fill in the RSVP form once with a test reply and submit.
3Check your inbox — you will receive an activation email from FormSubmit. Click the activation link.
4Done — every guest RSVP now arrives straight in your inbox. Free, no account needed.
⚠️ Do not skip step 3. Until you click the activation link, FormSubmit holds your test RSVP and nothing is delivered.

7. The venue map

The map under Venue is a live Google Map. To point it at your own venue, search your venue on Google Maps, then in index.html replace Beaumont+Manor in the map iframe address with your venue's name (spaces as + or %20).

8. Publish it free — GitHub Pages (about 5 minutes)

1Create a free account at github.com (skip if you have one).
2Click New repository — name it e.g. our-wedding, set it Public, click Create repository.
3Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in your whole unzipped site folder contents (index.html, styles.css, script.js, START-HERE.html, the images/ and audio/ folders). Click Commit changes.
4Go to Settings → Pages. Under "Build and deployment", choose Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder /(root) — click Save.
5Wait about a minute, refresh the Pages settings — your free link appears, like yourname.github.io/our-wedding. Share it with your guests!
💍 Want your own domain (e.g. vivienneandalexander.com)? Buy it from any registrar, then in the same Settings → Pages page enter it under "Custom domain" and add the DNS records GitHub shows you. The site itself needs no changes.
📱 Tip: open your published link on your phone — most guests will view it there. The layout is fully mobile-friendly.

9. FAQ

Can I change the fonts? Yes — they load from Google Fonts at the top of index.html. Swap Playfair+Display, Cormorant+Garamond, Great+Vibes or Jost for any Google Font names.

Can I remove a section? Yes — each section in index.html is labeled (<!-- ============ 5. SCHEDULE ============ --> etc.). Delete the block you don't need.

Can I change the music? Yes — replace audio/music.mp3 with your own MP3 (keep the same file name). It still plays only when a guest taps the ♪ button — never automatically.

Something broke? Re-download your original ZIP from Etsy and redo that step — you can't break anything permanently.
