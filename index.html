Chapter One

Our Story

It began, as the finest things do, with a glance across a crowded room — a spring charity ball, a shared laugh over the string quartet, and a dance that neither of them wanted to end.

What followed were stolen weekends, long letters, and Sunday mornings at the manor — Vivienne's laughter, Alexander's quiet certainty, and a thousand small moments that gathered, quietly, into a life.

And so, beneath the old oak at dusk — with a ring that had waited a century for its moment — Alexander asked, and Vivienne said yes.

A Note on Attire

Dress Code

Black Tie

Formal Evening Attire

  • Gentlemen — tuxedo, black bow tie, patent or polished dress shoes.
  • Ladies — floor-length gowns; an elegant cocktail dress in jewel tones is equally welcome.
  • Palette — burgundy, champagne, blush, ivory, and black are most welcome. We kindly ask that guests refrain from wearing white.
  • A practical note — the ceremony takes place in the rose garden; block heels are advised over stilettos.
  • Evening — September evenings can turn crisp; a wrap or tailored coat is recommended.
